Compare a garment without entering body sizes

Choose a clear, straight-overhead photo of a garment laid flat, with an A4 sheet or credit/debit card in the same plane. Mark the reference edge, then mark across the garment at the bust, waist and hip. The app estimates garment dimensions; it does not identify your body size or predict fit. You can skip the shopper-measurement panel for this comparison; its starting values are examples, and the photo path does not use them.

Private by design: your selected photo is read and measured in this browser only. It is not uploaded or saved by Fashion Fit.

Your photo appears here and stays on this device. Use two marks for each straight reference/garment width.

Choose a photo to begin. Keep the garment and reference flat, fully visible, and evenly lit.

No fabric is confirmed. The photo cannot reliably identify fabric.

Measurements are approximate: camera angle, wrinkles, garment stretch and mark placement affect them. A flat width is doubled to estimate circumference only when the garment is laid flat. Fabric is never inferred from appearance; check the care label or material with the seller.
